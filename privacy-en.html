Privacy Policy

Effective December 1, 2026

Boreum (“the app”) is a Korean lunar calendar you can use without signing up. This policy explains what information the app handles, where it is kept, and what information goes to Google for ads.

  • No sign-up, no loginThe app never asks for your name, email or phone number.
  • Your dates stay on your deviceAnniversaries, saved days and settings are stored on the device and never uploaded.
  • No server of oursThe developer does not receive or keep any of your information. There are no analytics tools in the app.
  • Ads by Google AdMobWhile you use the free plan, Google processes the information needed for ads. With Pro, the app stops requesting ads.

1. Information the developer collects

None. The developer does not collect any personal information, such as your name, contact details, date of birth, location or device identifiers, and the app sends nothing to the developer's own servers. The app contains no usage statistics or analytics tools.

The app never asks for a birth date, birth time or gender, and makes no readings about individuals. Calendar and almanac information is calculated from the date alone.

2. Information stored only on your device

The app keeps the following on your device only, so that its features work. None of it is sent to the developer.

Saved daysDate, title, note
AnniversariesTitle, type (birthday, memorial rite, anniversary, other), lunar or solar date and leap-month flag, year of origin (if you enter it), reminder timing, note
SettingsLanguage, text size, theme and appearance, calendar display options, reminder time, sound and haptics, and so on
App recordsWhether Pro is unlocked (a cached value for display), when and how often ads were shown (used only to keep ads infrequent), when a temporary pass from a rewarded ad expires, and a list of events exported to Calendar (to avoid duplicates)

Deleting the app deletes this information. If you use iCloud Backup or back up your device to a computer, it may be included in that device backup, which Apple manages.

Anniversary reminders include the anniversary's title, so depending on your iOS settings it may appear on the Lock Screen. To hide it, change Show Previews in iOS Settings › Notifications › Boreum.

3. Device features

Calendar (write-only)

The first time you tap “Add to Calendar”, the app asks for write-only calendar access. It adds only the events you choose (title, date, note, alert) to your default calendar and cannot read your existing events. Added events become part of Apple Calendar, and whether they sync depends on your calendar settings.

Notifications (local)

Anniversary and solar-term reminders are local notifications scheduled on the device; no push server is used. The app asks for permission once, when you first turn on a reminder. You can turn them off anytime in iOS Settings › Notifications › Boreum.

Sharing

The Share button opens the iOS share sheet. The information goes only to the app you choose; Boreum does not keep or send it anywhere else.

The app does not use location, photos, contacts, camera or microphone permissions.

4. Ads (Google AdMob)

While you use the free (Basic) plan, the Google Mobile Ads SDK from Google LLC shows ads. To select, show and measure ads, Google may collect and process the following through the app:

  • Device identifiers: the advertising identifier (IDFA, only if you allow tracking) or a device identifier scoped to the app or developer
  • Coarse location: an approximate area estimated from your IP address (no precise location)
  • Advertising data: such as the ads you saw and tapped
  • Product interaction: such as app launches and ad-related taps
  • Diagnostics: performance data and crash logs from the ads SDK

Google handles this information under How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency)

On first launch, iOS asks once whether to allow tracking. Every feature works the same if you decline, and ads are then shown without the advertising identifier (IDFA). You can change your answer anytime in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. The app asks this before it requests any ads, and the advertising identifier is not used unless you allow tracking.

Ad consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and similar regions)

Where the law requires consent, Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) appears before any ad is requested. To check this, the app contacts Google's servers at launch, and Google determines your region from your IP address. Users in those regions can change or withdraw their choice anytime from the ad privacy option that appears in the app's Settings.

Ad rating and Pro

  • Ads are limited to content suitable for all ages (rating G).
  • After you buy Pro, the app stops requesting ads, and from the next launch it does not start the ads SDK at all. A temporary pass earned from a rewarded ad does not remove ads.

5. International transfer of ad-related information

The ad-related information in section 4 is collected directly by Google and processed outside Korea. As required by Korea's Personal Information Protection Act, here are the details.

RecipientGoogle LLC (contact: Google privacy help)
CountriesThe United States and other countries where Google processes data
ItemsDevice identifiers (IDFA if allowed), coarse location estimated from IP address, advertising data, product interaction, diagnostics
When and howSent over the network when the free app checks ad consent or requests and shows ads
PurposeServing and measuring ads, preventing fraud, improving the ad service
RetentionUnder Google's Privacy Policy
How to refuseDecline tracking (stops IDFA use); in consent regions, withdraw consent from the ad privacy option; or buy Pro (stops ad requests). The app's core features keep working either way.

6. In-app purchase

Pro is sold through Apple's App Store, which processes the payment. The developer never receives payment details such as card numbers; the app only checks whether Pro was purchased, using Apple's StoreKit. Restoring a purchase also goes through your Apple Account.

7. Children

The app is for a general audience and is not directed at children. The developer does not collect personal information from anyone, including children, and ads are limited to content suitable for all ages (G). Parents can use iOS Screen Time to restrict in-app purchases and tracking requests.

8. Retention and deletion

The developer keeps no user information. You can delete anniversaries and saved days in the app, and deleting the app removes everything stored on the device. Ad-related information processed by Google is kept and deleted under Google's policies.

9. Your rights

Because the developer holds no information about you, there is nothing on our side to access, correct or delete. You can view, edit and delete the information on your device within the app. You can change your ad choices as described in section 4, and exercise rights over information held by Google under Google's policies. If you have questions, contact us below.

10. Security

The app does not send your records off the device, and information stored on the device is protected by iOS data protection (device passcode and encryption). Since the developer receives no user information, there is no separate system that stores it.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the new version and its effective date on this page, and mention significant changes in the app's update notes.
